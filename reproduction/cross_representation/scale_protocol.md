# Sensitivity to matching the original square-kernel diagonal

This follow-up to release r3 and to the review of 19 November 2026 was specified after seeing the existing fixed, selected and unit-diagonal results, and before calculating the new matched-full-kernel predictions. It uses all ten archived DTD splits and all four held-out-specimen splits of KTH-TIPS2-b. Both datasets were familiar. This is an exploratory sensitivity, with no new data collection or external preregistration.

Keep the equal three block weights, the original square-plus-linear kernel, its ridge penalty of one, the full-image features, splits and all training-fold preprocessing. For each representation and outer training set, scale the full quadratic kernel by the ratio of the mean training diagonal of the square kernel to that of the full kernel. Scale its training and test-to-training matrices by the same factor. The test set plays no role in setting the factor. Fit all models with the class prior and centred targets used in r3. Count zero blocks separately in training and test, measure mean and individual diagonal agreement, and check the equality of scores obtained using the unscaled full kernel with the reciprocal penalty. For nonzero unit-length blocks the theoretical diagonals are 9/13 and 11/13 and the theoretical reciprocal penalty is 11/9.

Record paired square, native full and matched full predictions, absolute recalls, per-representation gains and the DINOv2-minus-colour–texture difference in gains. Report pointwise 95% intervals for the individual gains, and simultaneous 95% intervals for the two matched representation contrasts using Bonferroni across DTD and KTH. Changes from the native-scale contrast are descriptive. Preserve exact-image grouping across repeated DTD test appearances and resample whole specimens within KTH materials, using the 100,000-draw procedures and seed 19112026 from r3. Inference conditions on the fitted classifiers. The separate native-scale model-selection experiment retains its original grid and results.

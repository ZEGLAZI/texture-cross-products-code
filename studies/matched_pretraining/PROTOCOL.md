# Locked comparison of block-similarity products

The three existing tasks, including their supervised ResNet-50 and DINOv2 results, and the retrospective DTD penalty contrast were inspected previously. The DeepFashion-MultiModal classifier outputs and both original DINO checkpoint results were unseen when this specification was written. This is a timestamped internal design, without an independent preregistration.

DeepFashion-MultiModal. Use the authors' pattern_ann.txt, manual parsing masks and catalogue photographs. Select a reliable association between catalogue group, attribute column and parsing label. Top: Tees_Tanks, Graphic_Tees, Blouses_Shirts, Shirts_Polos, Sweaters, Sweatshirts_Hoodies (upper, mask 1); jackets/vests/coats (outer, mask 2); Skirts (lower, mask 3), Pants/Shorts/Denim (lower, mask 5), Leggings (lower, mask 6). Exclude ambiguous categories including dresses, rompers, cardigans and suiting. Retain floral, graphic, striped, pure colour, lattice and colour block where at least 25 eligible independent product IDs are available per motif. Exclude other, NA and conflicting valid named patterns within each catalogue product, across all annotated views of its referenced garment. Product IDs span colourways and camera views. Exclude products with gender/category disagreement. A photo requires an original manual mask aligned in size with the source. Use the largest odd square fully inside the relevant label, minimum 65 pixels, first row-major centre on ties; use all its RGB pixels, then bicubically resize to 224. Remove duplicate pixels; exclude products implicated in cross-product exact or near duplicates among eligible crops, or matches to accessible earlier images. Near duplicate screen: 64-bit DCT perceptual hash (Hamming <=4) plus mean absolute RGB thumbnail difference <=8 on the 0--255 scale; this screen is incomplete for viewpoint or stronger colour changes. Document scope. Limit each product to two eligible views and each named class to 600 products ordered by SHA-256 of deterministic text keys, to bound unequal representation and fitting cost. Reserve 35% of products in each motif for one test and 20% for inner validation (floor, minima one); refit on all other products. No folds or replacement datasets chosen by observed scores. Freeze the realized manifest and aggregate counts before any external classification.

Primary external endpoint: per model, average correctness across views of each product, across products of each motif, then equally across motifs. Also give image-level macro recall. Apply the original equal-weight square kernel S and full kernel Q to handcrafted wavelet/RGB, frozen DINOv2 ViT-S/14, supervised ResNet-50, and the two original DINO models below. Retain a common lambda=1, a training-mean diagonal sensitivity of Q, and separate validation using the existing grid (1, .1, 10, .01, 100, .001), with ties in this order. External validation uses the same product-balanced score as its primary endpoint. Train-only scaling, the original prior-centred kernel ridge fit and common crops for every model. Primary T = (gain_ResNet-supervised - gain_DINOv2)_selected minus the same contrast at lambda=1, where gain=Q-S. A positive direction is suggested by DTD. Publish the estimate and 95% two-sided percentile interval, conditional on fitted models, using 100,000 paired resamples of products within each test motif, seed 20261120. Include every other endpoint as descriptive unless specified below.

Matched pretraining. DINO ResNet-50 and DINO ViT-S/16, official ImageNet-1K weights from Caron et al. Use exactly the input tensors of the published models, ImageNet normalization and frozen eval mode. Pool ResNet layer2/layer3/layer4 spatially. For ViT-S/16 take stages 3/6/12, apply the official final LayerNorm, discard CLS, globally average the remaining 196 tokens. Do not search over architectures, depths, pooling, kernels or penalties. Use the original DTD/KTH/FMD-to-Lytro partitions, inputs and within-training normalization, and the new garment protocol. Define q=(Q-S)_selected minus (Q-S)_native. Secondary contrasts A=q_DINO-ResNet minus q_supervised-ResNet and B=q_DINO-ResNet minus q_DINO-ViT. Use the manuscript's grouped, conditional paired inference for each corpus and a single Bonferroni family of eight (two contrasts on four tasks). Secondary shared-family comparison does not hold every training hyperparameter fixed. Report absolute S/Q recalls and native/matched/selected gains. Never infer differences across tasks from separate intervals.

All outputs will be retained. Summarize the external primary and the two matched comparisons in the paper, with all settings, class counts, comparisons and limitations in the supplement and reviewer materials. Reproduce new predictions from features and check the mapping to reported values.
